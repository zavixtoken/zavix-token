# Zavix (ZVX) Development Roadmap

The Zavix roadmap represents development objectives rather than guaranteed dates. Priorities may evolve based on technical review, community feedback and ecosystem opportunities.

## Phase 1 — Foundation

- Token deployment on BNB Smart Chain
- Smart contract verification
- Official Zavix website
- Core project documentation
- Public token information and transparency resources

## Phase 2 — Community & Visibility

- Establish official communication channels
- Publish transparent project information
- Improve user resources
- Expand the project's public presence and documentation

## Phase 3 — Ecosystem Development

- Explore practical applications for Zavix
- Explore ecosystem integrations
- Explore additional liquidity opportunities
- Explore additional exchange opportunities where appropriate

## Phase 4 — Expansion

- Continue development based on technical review
- Respond to community feedback
- Explore suitable ecosystem opportunities
- Expand the project according to practical and technical requirements

## Roadmap Disclaimer

The roadmap describes development objectives and does not represent guaranteed dates, results, exchange listings, liquidity levels, token prices or future returns.

Development priorities may change as the Zavix project evolves.
